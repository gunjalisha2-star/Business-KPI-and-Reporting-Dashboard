# Business KPI and Reporting Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Business KPI and Reporting Dashboard** is an interactive dashboard designed to analyze business performance, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and identify revenue and profitability trends. It provides a consolidated view of sales performance, product categories, country-wise revenue, and revenue distribution through different visualizations and interactive filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor key business KPIs, including total revenue, total profit, gross profit margin, units sold, orders, and cost of goods sold.
- Analyze monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Identify top-performing countries based on revenue.
- Understand revenue distribution across different sales channels.
- Explore business performance using interactive filters.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue and Profit Analysis
- Monthly revenue trend analysis.
- Comparison of total revenue and gross profit.
- Identification of monthly performance patterns.

### 2. Country-Wise Revenue Analysis
- Horizontal bar chart comparing revenue across countries.
- Identification of top-performing markets.
- Comparison of revenue contribution by country.

### 3. Revenue Share by Channel
- Donut and pie charts showing revenue distribution.
- Comparison of sales channels and regional contributions.
- Visualization of each channel's share of total revenue.

### 4. Geographic Revenue Analysis
- Map visualization of revenue across countries.
- Helps identify geographical sales patterns.

### 5. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:
- Product Category
- Country
- Quarter

These filters allow users to explore specific segments and analyze business performance from different perspectives.
